Build a web app supporting cancer patients based on the nine factors from "Radical Remission" research. The app should combine practical tools with AI guidance while complementing conventional treatment.

## Core Features

### User System & Medical Tracking
- Secure profiles with medical history input
- Document upload for test results with AI interpretation
- Progress tracking dashboard with visualizations

### AI Health Assistant
- Chatbot trained on oncology research and wellness practices
- Personalized recommendations based on diagnosis
- Daily/weekly guidance with empathetic tone

### Nutrition Center
- Anti-inflammatory diet plans and recipes
- Shopping list generator
- Meal planning and nutrition tracking

### Mind-Body Tools
- Meditation, breathing exercises, and stress-reduction techniques
- Emotion tracking with sentiment analysis
- Sleep optimization resources

### Movement & Exercise
- Customized exercise plans for different treatment stages
- Video library of appropriate physical activities
- Activity tracking based on energy levels

### Supplements & Therapies
- Evidence-based supplement database
- Interaction checker with conventional treatments
- Therapy educational resources

### Community Support
- Moderated forums by cancer type
- Mentor matching with survivors
- Resource sharing capabilities

### Spiritual & Psychological Wellbeing
- Reflective practices and purpose discovery tools
- CBT-inspired exercises and mood tracking
- AI support for emotional wellbeing

### Integrated Calendar
- Holistic scheduling for appointments, nutrition, and wellness
- Reminder system with motivational messages
- Treatment and activity coordination

## Technical Requirements
- React frontend, Node/Express backend, MongoDB
- OpenAI API integration for chatbot
- Calendar API integration
- HIPAA-compliant data handling
- Mobile-responsive design

## Safety & Ethical Considerations
- Clear disclaimers about complementary nature
- Language about consulting healthcare providers
- Evidence-based information with citations
- Privacy-first data handling
- Medical professional review process
